# Panel Reader privacy policy — draft

Prepared 9 October 2026 for the version 0.1.0 starter. Review for the final
release, add a public support contact and publish before App Store submission.

## Your reading data

Panel Reader stores imported PDFs and images, book titles, favourites,
bookmarks, reading progress and history in the app's storage on your device.
Appearance and reading-layout preferences, saved website shortcuts, each
website's last successfully loaded URL and web page bookmarks are also stored
on the device.
This information supports your personal library and reading experience.

The current version does not upload your personal library or saved links to
the developer. Panel Reader does not require its own account, include a native
advertising or analytics SDK, or operate a developer-run server or cloud sync.

## Website browsing

Websites open only when you choose to visit them. Your browser requests are sent
to those websites and to services used by their pages. They can receive your
IP address, browser information, requested URLs and information you choose to
submit, and they may display ads or use analytics. A private session does not
prevent a website from receiving requests or recording activity on its servers.
Each website's privacy policy and terms govern that activity.

The browser uses a separate, nonpersistent WebKit data store for each session.
Website cookies, caches and local website storage are cleared when that browser
closes. Signing in on a website does not create a Panel Reader account. The app
does not use a script bridge to read website passwords or form fields.

The app saves website names and URLs, a resume URL for each saved website, and
the title and URL of pages you explicitly bookmark. These records stay on your
device. Websites do not receive your imported books or local book metadata from
the app. If you share a page with the system share sheet, the service you select
handles the shared URL under its own policies.

## File selection and backups

You select files using Apple's Files interface. When a file comes from a
cloud provider, iOS and that provider handle retrieving it under their
policies. Panel Reader copies the selected file into its local app storage.

Your device settings may include app data in device or iCloud backups
managed by Apple. Such backups are separate from the app's functionality.

## Removing data

Use **Delete book** in the library to remove the imported copy and reading
record. Remove a website or saved page from its context menu in **Sources** to
delete its saved link. Removing a website leaves separately saved page bookmarks
available until you remove them. Close its browser to clear the session's local
website data; request deletion of website accounts or server records from the
website operator. The original imported file remains in its original location. Deleting the
app removes its local app storage. Existing backups may persist according
to your backup settings and provider's retention policies.

## Changes and contact

Update this policy if the app adds native online catalogues, downloads, accounts,
cloud sync, analytics or other data processing. Before publishing, replace
this paragraph with a monitored support contact and an effective date.
