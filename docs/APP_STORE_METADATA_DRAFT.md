## Description

Bring your own books in PDF or image format and make time for your next chapter.

Panel Reader lets you organise a personal reading library on iPhone and iPad.
Import a PDF, or select image pages to create a book. Find a title with search,
save favourites and continue from your last page.

- Read vertically or switch to horizontal pages.
- Zoom into PDF pages and enlarge image pages.
- Bookmark pages to return to them later.
- Find recently opened books in reading history.
- Choose a dark appearance for your library.

Imported books, bookmarks and reading progress are stored on your device.
Start with Night Train, the original six-page sample included in the app,
or import your own files from Files.
